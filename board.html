How scoring works

Each day, everyone logs their three Fermi multipliers. Players are ranked by the average of the three (lower is better). Points are awarded by rank — in a field of five, first place earns five points, second earns four, and so on, with ties sharing rank and points. The weekly board sums points Monday to Sunday; all‑time is the running total.
